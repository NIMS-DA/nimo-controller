We will run an experiment that maps a phase diagram. Perform exactly 20 experimental cycles in total. In each of the first 10 cycles, choose the next candidate using the selection tool with the RE (random exploration) method. In each of the remaining 10 cycles, choose the next candidate using the selection tool with the PDC (phase diagram construction) method. In every one of the 20 cycles, after the candidate is proposed, evaluate the get_phase tool with the proposed values of temperature and pressure and register the returned value as the objective via an update. After all 20 cycles are finished, plot the phase diagram.
